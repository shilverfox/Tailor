# Tailor 30s App Preview Storyboard

Status: draft for production
Target market: United States
Primary use: App Store Preview, portrait
Secondary use: TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts cutdown
Product position: private pet memory books for people who lost a dog or cat

## Creative strategy

Tailor should feel warm, private, and useful within the first three seconds. The video must make one promise clearly:

> Create a private memory book for a pet you loved, using real photos you choose.

Avoid grief exploitation. Do not imply therapy, healing, contact with a deceased pet, AI-written emotions, disease inference, death-cause inference, or cloud AI. The conversion driver is trust plus tangible output: a beautiful editable book, single-page sharing, and a print-ready PDF.

## Message hierarchy

1. This is for a pet you loved.
2. It uses real photos from your iPhone.
3. Nothing is uploaded.
4. You approve what enters the book.
5. Tailor creates an editable first draft.
6. You choose a style and review pages.
7. You can share a page or export the complete print-ready PDF.

## 30-second App Store Preview

| Time | Visual | On-screen copy | Notes |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.5 | iPhone over warm paper background. Tailor welcome screen appears. Subtle push-in. | `For the pet you loved.` | Must be readable with audio muted. Open with product category, not abstract emotion. |
| 2.5–5.0 | Profile screen: species, name, Came Home, Left Us. | `Start with their name.` | Use a fictional pet name such as Milo. Do not show real user data. |
| 5.0–7.5 | Reference photo picker / selected reference photos. | `Choose a few real photos.` | Communicates low setup effort. |
| 7.5–10.5 | On-device scan and review grid. Include / exclude actions briefly visible. | `Tailor finds moments on your iPhone.` | Use actual app UI. If using mock photos, they must look like ordinary pet photos, not fantasy art. |
| 10.5–13.0 | Privacy card or privacy screen: on-device, not uploaded. | `Nothing is uploaded.` | This is a key trust moment. Hold long enough to read. |
| 13.0–16.5 | Smart Draft progress / story plan. | `A first draft from photos you approve.` | Avoid "AI writes your story." |
| 16.5–20.5 | Book style screen. Quick cuts through Warm Paper, Quiet Night, Field Notes. | `Choose a style that feels right.` | Show visual variety and quality. This is the strongest emotional/value beat. |
| 20.5–23.5 | Page editor / preview. Tap Customize, show one page edit or crop/text adjustment. | `Edit every page.` | Demonstrates control and trust. |
| 23.5–26.5 | Final Preview toolbar with `Share page`; share sheet begins to rise or tap animation only. | `Share a remembrance page.` | Use only if unlocked Final Preview UI is shown. Do not replace PDF value. |
| 26.5–29.0 | Export screen: `Your PDF is ready.` and `Save and share PDF`. | `Export a print-ready PDF.` | This is the paid value moment. |
| 29.0–30.0 | App icon + closing card. | `Tailor · Private Pet Memory Books` | CTA: `Try it on iPhone`. |

## Visual direction

- Format: portrait 9:16.
- Recommended App Store sizes: export at accepted iPhone portrait resolution from Apple specs for the selected device class.
- Frame rate: 30 fps.
- Music: soft piano, felt piano, or restrained ambient pad.
- Color: Tailor paper / ink / clay palette.
- Motion: slow push-ins, page turns, gentle crossfades. Avoid fast tech-demo pacing.
- Typography: clean SF-style captions; keep each caption under two lines.
- No voiceover required for App Store Preview. If used for social, voiceover should be calm and factual.

## Voiceover option for social only

```text
When a pet leaves, the photos stay.
Tailor helps you turn them into a private memory book,
made from real moments you choose.
It stays on your iPhone.
You can edit every page,
share one remembrance,
or export the complete PDF.
```

Do not use this voiceover for App Store Preview unless it is paired with full captions and app footage remains primary.

## Conversion rationale

- The first line identifies the emotional job to be done.
- The privacy line removes the biggest objection for sensitive pet photos.
- The "photos you approve" line distinguishes Tailor from generative grief content.
- Template and preview shots create perceived quality.
- Page sharing creates immediate social value.
- PDF export creates a clear reason to unlock.

## Claims to avoid

- `AI heals grief`
- `Bring them back`
- `Never forget again`
- `We know their story`
- `Automatically writes their life`
- `Share with family through a link`
- `Cloud backup`
- Any medical, therapeutic, bereavement-treatment, or spiritual claim
