# Tailor Marketing Video Editor Brief

Status: draft for designer / editor
Primary deliverable: 30s portrait App Store Preview
Secondary deliverables: 15s social cutdown, 6s bumper, poster frame

## Product in one sentence

Tailor helps someone turn real photos of a dog or cat they loved into a private, editable memory book, with single-page sharing and a print-ready PDF export.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- US iPhone users.
- Dog or cat owners who have lost a pet.
- They have many photos in Apple Photos and want something more meaningful than scrolling an album.
- They are privacy-sensitive and do not want to upload all pet photos to a cloud service.

Secondary audience:

- Family members making a remembrance gift.
- People who want a printable keepsake but do not want to design a book manually from scratch.

## Emotional tone

Warm, quiet, practical. The viewer should feel:

- "This understands the moment."
- "It will not misuse my photos."
- "I can control what goes into the book."
- "The result looks beautiful enough to pay for."

Avoid:

- heavy grief music;
- manipulative sadness;
- spiritual claims;
- AI magic language;
- stock-photo families crying;
- aggressive purchase messaging.

## Creative guardrails

Do:

- show real app UI;
- show real-looking pet photos;
- show the privacy/on-device promise;
- show user approval and editing;
- show beautiful templates;
- show final PDF value.

Do not:

- claim Tailor writes memories or understands grief;
- show cloud upload;
- show accounts, collaboration, links, or printing services;
- show any feature not in the shipping app;
- show personal contact names in share sheets;
- overstate "healing" or therapeutic impact.

## Structure

### Opening hook

The first frame must make the product category obvious:

```text
For the pet you loved.
```

Do not open with generic product UI or abstract nature footage. If the viewer sees only one second, they should understand this is a pet memory-book app.

### Middle

Build trust and capability:

1. Real photos.
2. On-device scan.
3. User approves.
4. Draft becomes editable book.
5. Templates make it beautiful.

### Ending

End on value:

1. Share one page.
2. Export complete PDF.
3. Tailor brand card.

## Caption style

- White or Tailor ink text depending on background.
- Use rounded, readable sans-serif.
- Maximum two lines.
- Keep each caption visible at least 1.3 seconds.
- Avoid small legal-like copy in the video.
- Add soft shadow or translucent paper card behind captions if needed.

Approved caption set:

```text
For the pet you loved.
Start with their name.
Choose a few real photos.
Tailor finds moments on your iPhone.
Nothing is uploaded.
A first draft from photos you approve.
Choose a style that feels right.
Edit every page.
Share a remembrance page.
Export a print-ready PDF.
Tailor · Private Pet Memory Books
```

## Music and sound

App Store Preview may autoplay muted, so the edit must work silently.

If music is included:

- soft piano, felt piano, or warm ambient;
- no vocals;
- no dramatic grief swell;
- loop or resolve gently at 30s;
- confirm commercial/App Store usage rights.

Optional social voiceover:

```text
When a pet leaves, the photos stay.
Tailor helps you turn them into a private memory book,
made from real moments you choose.
It stays on your iPhone.
You can edit every page,
share one remembrance,
or export the complete PDF.
```

## Deliverables

### D1 — App Store Preview

- Duration: 30s maximum.
- Orientation: portrait.
- Frame rate: 30fps.
- Format: `.mp4` or `.mov`.
- Must work muted.
- Must be built from app UI footage.

### D2 — Social cutdown

- Duration: 15s.
- Orientation: portrait.
- Can use faster montage and stronger hook.
- May include voiceover.

### D3 — Bumper

- Duration: 6s.
- Single product promise and one polished app/result shot.

### D4 — Poster frame

Recommended poster frame:

```text
For the pet you loved.
Tailor
Private Pet Memory Books
```

Use a beautiful Final Preview page or welcome hero, not a purchase screen.

## Acceptance criteria

- Viewer understands within 3 seconds that Tailor is for pet memory books.
- Viewer understands by 12 seconds that photos stay private/on-device.
- Viewer sees at least one actual book page by 20 seconds.
- Viewer sees both share-page and PDF export value by the end.
- No non-shipping feature is shown as available.
- No sensitive real-user data or personal contact information appears.
- Captions remain readable on a phone screen.
- The final frame contains Tailor and `Private Pet Memory Books`.

## Open decisions for product owner

- Whether to show the one-time purchase screen in the App Store Preview. Recommendation: no; show value, not price.
- Whether to include voiceover in social versions. Recommendation: yes for social, no for App Store Preview.
- Whether demo pet is dog-only or dog plus cat. Recommendation: dog in 30s main preview; create a cat-specific social variant later.
- Whether the closing CTA says `Try it on iPhone` or `Create a memory book`. Recommendation: `Try it on iPhone` for App Store, `Create a memory book` for ads.
