Frequently asked questions

Are my photos private?

Yes. Tailor analyzes and stores everything on your iPhone. Your photos are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

How do I change the album template or style?

Open your book, then use the Style control at the bottom of the preview to pick a different template and paper size.

How do I export or share my book?

From the preview, tap Generate full PDF. After the one-time unlock you can save and share a print-ready PDF.

I unlocked the album on another device. How do I restore it?

On the purchase screen, tap Restore purchase. Your unlock is tied to your Apple Account.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Delete all local Tailor data. This removes Tailor's data from your device. Your original photos in Apple Photos are never touched.

Tailor can't find my pet's photos.

Make sure Tailor has photo access (Settings → Manage photo access), and add a few clear reference photos of your pet so recognition has more to work with.
