Tailor

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

The short version. Tailor is a private, on-device app. Your photos are analyzed and stored only on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to us or anyone else, and we do not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

Who this policy is for

This policy explains how the Tailor app ("Tailor," "we," "us") handles your information. By using Tailor, you agree to the practices described here.

What Tailor does with your photos

Tailor helps you turn photos of a pet you love into a private memory book or keepsake. All of this happens locally on your device:

  • Photo analysis and recognition run entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device frameworks (PhotoKit and Vision).
  • Your photo library is never uploaded to our servers or any third party. Tailor has no photo-upload service.
  • Only the photos you explicitly confirm are included in your book. Automated matches are suggestions you review and approve.

What we collect

We do not collect any personal data. Tailor has no account system, no analytics SDK, and no advertising. We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not build a profile of you.

Where your data is stored

Your pet profiles, review decisions, memory-book edits, and exported PDFs are stored locally on your device. They remain under your control and are not transmitted to us.

Photo library permission

Tailor requests access to your photo library only to find and organize pictures of your pet. You can grant full or limited access, and you can change or revoke this permission at any time in the iOS Settings app. Denying access does not send any data to us — it simply limits what Tailor can help you organize.

Deleting your data

You can delete Tailor's local data at any time from within the app (Settings → "Delete all local Tailor data"). This removes Tailor's pet profiles, photo matches, decisions, edits, and generated PDFs from your device. It does not delete the original photographs in your Apple Photos library. Deleting the app also removes its local data.

Purchases

Tailor offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock the complete book export. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Tailor is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
